EquiformerV3 — The Full Picture

What goes in, what comes out, and how spherical harmonics make it all work.

One sentence: EquiformerV3 takes atomic positions + element types, builds a graph, represents interactions using spherical harmonics (so rotation equivariance is exact), and predicts the system's potential energy and per-atom forces.

1. Inputs — What the model sees

The input is an atomic structure — a collection of atoms with positions in 3D space, optionally inside a periodic unit cell (crystal).

FieldShapeMeaning
pos[N, 3]xyz coordinates of each atom (Ångströms)
atomic_numbers[N]Element type (Na=11, Cl=17, C=6, …)
cell[3, 3]Periodic unit cell vectors (for crystals)
batch[N]Which sample each atom belongs to (batching)
natoms[B]Atom count per sample in the batch

That's it. No hand-crafted features. Raw geometry + element identity.

Figure 1. An atomic structure becomes a graph: atoms within a cutoff radius are connected by edges.

2. Spherical Harmonics — The representation

What are they?

Spherical harmonics are a basis for functions on the sphere — like Fourier modes, but for angular patterns instead of periodic signals. They come in levels of increasing angular detail:

Figure 2. Each level l has (2l+1) components and captures finer angular structure.

Why the model uses them

The key property: spherical harmonics transform predictably under rotation. If you rotate the molecule, the l=0 component stays the same (it's a scalar), the l=1 components mix among themselves via a 3×3 matrix, l=2 via a 5×5 matrix, etc. These mixing matrices are Wigner-D matrices.

This means equivariance is algebraically guaranteed — not learned from data augmentation.

Node features in EquiformerV3:
x.shape = [N_atoms, (lmax+1)², num_channels]

For lmax=6: each atom carries 49 spherical harmonic coefficients × 128 channels.
The middle dimension is the spherical harmonic basis: indices 0 = l=0, indices 1–3 = l=1, indices 4–8 = l=2, …

4. Outputs — What the model predicts

OutputShapeHow computedPhysical meaning
Energy [B] l=0 features → MLP → sum over atoms Total potential energy (eV)
Forces [N, 3] l=1 features → equivariant attention head, or −∂E/∂pos Force on each atom (eV/Å)
Stress [B, 3, 3] ∂E/∂strain or direct head from l=2 Stress tensor on unit cell (eV/ų)

Why the outputs have different symmetry types

Energy is a scalar (l=0)

Rotating the molecule doesn't change its energy. The model reads this from the l=0 (invariant) component of node features.

Forces are vectors (l=1)

Rotating the molecule rotates the forces identically. The model reads this from the l=1 (vector) component — equivariance is automatic.

5. What "energy" means physically

The predicted energy is the potential energy surface (PES) — the total electronic energy of the atomic configuration as computed by quantum mechanics (DFT). This is what density functional theory calculates at enormous cost (~hours per structure for large systems).

Why it matters:
  • Energy → stability (lower = more stable configuration)
  • Forces = −∇E → how atoms want to move → molecular dynamics simulations
  • Stress = ∂E/∂strain → how the cell wants to deform → pressure calculations

The model replaces DFT: same accuracy, ~10⁶× faster. This enables simulations at time/length scales previously impossible.

6. Training labels

Training data comes from millions of DFT calculations (OC20, OMat24, MPTrj datasets):

LabelSourceLoss
Energy (eV)DFT total energyMAE or Huber
Forces (eV/Å)DFT forces (−∂E_DFT/∂pos)MAE per component
Stress (eV/ų)DFT stress tensorMAE
 
loss = λ_E · |E_pred − E_dft| + λ_F · mean(|F_pred − F_dft|) + λ_σ · |σ_pred − σ_dft|

Typical weighting: forces dominate (λ_F ≫ λ_E) because force accuracy matters most for dynamics.

7. The role of each component

ComponentWhat it doesWhy spherical harmonics matter here
edge_rot_mat Builds a local coordinate frame per edge Defines the z-axis for evaluating SH along each edge
Wigner-D Rotation matrices for SH coefficients Converts between edge-local and global frames
Tensor product Couples features of different l orders The only bilinear op that preserves equivariance (CG selection rules)
SO(2) conv Efficient alternative to full tensor product Exploits m-structure within each l to reduce cost from O(l³) to O(l²)
Attention Learns which neighbors matter most Scores from l=0 (invariant) so "how much" doesn't depend on orientation
Energy head Scalar MLP on l=0 features l=0 is rotationally invariant → energy is invariant ✓
Force head Reads l=1 features directly l=1 transforms like a vector → forces rotate correctly ✓

Bottom line. Spherical harmonics are not an optional feature of EquiformerV3 — they ARE the representation. Every feature, every message, every output lives in this basis. The entire architecture is designed around the fact that spherical harmonics have known, exact transformation rules under rotation. Remove them and you have a fundamentally different (and less capable) model.
